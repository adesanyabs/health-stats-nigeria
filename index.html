Data checks

We compared the dashboard with the 2023-24 Nigeria national health survey final report (FR395).

National figures: all five statistics match the report.

All 37 states: Penta vaccination and children who die before age 5 match exactly.

Where the sources differ: the survey data service differed from the report by up to about 4 points for check-ups in pregnancy, births attended by a doctor or nurse/midwife, and births in a clinic or hospital. For 2024 state values of those three we use the report (Tables 9.2, 9.10 and 9.8).

What that means: earlier years still come from the data service, so state changes since 2018 for those three are approximate.

Children who die before age 5: state and zone rates cover 10 years before the survey, the national rate covers 5 years, so they are not directly comparable.

Methods and limits

Surveys run every few years, so figures are not current monthly data.

A warning marks figures based on fewer than 50 people. State figures are less certain than national ones, and most have no published confidence range.

Malaria surveys (2010, 2021) use a different sample from the national health survey, so we never join them to its trend lines.

State figures exist from 2013. Earlier years show zones only, and the 1990 regions are not shown.
